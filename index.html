What actually drives views on educational Reels?

I coded 300 educational Instagram Reels from 21 accounts on 30+ variables and compared each Reel with others from the same account. The account explained 68% of the difference in views, while no hook type, length or editing pace was reliably linked to them. I turned the findings into a free toolkit for educational creators.
